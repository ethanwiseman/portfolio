# Supersonic Rocket Analysis

[Back to portfolio](../../README.md)

**Tools:** Ansys Fluent and Ansys Mechanical  
**Focus:** Aerodynamics, thermal loading, and structural response

## Objective

Investigate aerodynamic loading and temperature behavior around simplified rocket components, then use CFD insights to examine structural and thermal response.

## My contribution

- Prepared simplified cone and tail geometry and surrounding flow domains.
- Used Ansys Fluent to examine the flow solution and solver residual behavior.
- Used Ansys Mechanical to evaluate stress and temperature distributions informed by the CFD analysis.
- Examined elevated stress and heat near the tail and considered implications for material durability.

## Analysis workflow

1. Define geometry, flow domains, and operating conditions.
2. Generate a mesh and configure the CFD model.
3. Review residual behavior and relevant flow outputs.
4. Evaluate structural and thermal response using the CFD insights.
5. Interpret concentrated loads and heating in relation to the geometry and material assumptions.

Steel was considered in the project. Material grade, properties, and operating conditions should accompany the original results when uploaded.

## Engineering reasoning

Aerodynamic pressure and heating influence different aspects of component behavior. Examining them alongside stress and temperature distributions helps identify locations that need further design attention.

Residual review was part of the work; residual reduction alone does not establish mesh independence or physical validation. The original setup and results are needed before reporting quantitative conclusions.
